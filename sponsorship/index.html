About Us

What We Do

The McMaster Economics Society (MES) is a student-led organization passionate about connecting students through economics. We welcome students from all programs to engage in events that foster shared interests, academic growth, and professional development. Our events give sponsors a direct way to engage with student talent and build a visible presence within the McMaster community.

Our Reach

Through our events, social media platforms, website, and newsletter, we are connecting with more students and creating more opportunities for organizations to engage with the McMaster community. Over the past year, MES has:

  • Attracted 400+ attendees
  • Connected students with 50+ industry professionals
  • Built a community of 1,500+ followers across Instagram and LinkedIn

Main Events

Case Study Competition

Our Case Study Competition gives students the chance to apply what they have learned to a real-world business challenge and present their solutions to industry professionals. Sponsorship helps us provide the resources that make the competition possible, while giving your organization the opportunity to play a direct role in the experience.

Alumni Networking

Alumni Networking creates a space for students to meet McMaster alumni working across different industries and build lasting connections. Sponsorship helps us bring more students and alumni together while giving your organization a meaningful presence in the connections being formed.

Economics Pathways

Rebranded from Econ Talks

Economics Pathways helps students explore the academic and career opportunities available through an economics degree. With sponsor support, we can introduce students to a wider range of industries while giving your organization the chance to connect with students early in degree.
